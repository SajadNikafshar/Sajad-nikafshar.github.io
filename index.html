About

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Forestry at Michigan State University, working as a Graduate Research Assistant in the laboratory of Prof. Mojgan Nejad. My research focuses on converting renewable, bio-based feedstocks into high-performance materials that can replace petroleum-derived chemicals in industrial applications.

I hold B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Chemical Engineering, which give my work a strong grounding in process design and reaction engineering. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in December 2027.

Research

Bio-based epoxy resins

Most epoxy resins in use today are derived from petroleum. My research develops lignin-based epoxy resins as renewable alternatives for coatings, adhesives, and composites, with the goal of matching or exceeding the performance of conventional systems.
